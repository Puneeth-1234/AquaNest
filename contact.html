Everything you need to know about purchasing and caring for live fish online.

Our specimens are gently fasted 24 hours prior to packing to minimize waste. Each fish is individually placed in triple-sealed breather bags infused with medical-grade pure oxygen and aquatic anti-stress conditioners. The bags are nestled into custom expanded polystyrene (EPS) insulated thermal boxes along with cooling ice-packs in summer or warming pads in winter. We ship via express priority air couriers strictly between Monday and Thursday to ensure zero weekend depot delays.
We take full financial and ethical responsibility for our fish during transit. In the rare event a specimen arrives deceased, simply send two clear photographs of the fish inside the unopened shipping bag to warranty@aquanest.in or WhatsApp +91 98765 43210 within 2 hours of delivery receipt. We will process an immediate free replacement or 100% refund to your original payment method.
Turn off intense aquarium lights to reduce stress. Float the sealed transport bag in your aquarium for 20 minutes to equalize water temperature. Next, open the bag and slowly add 1/4 cup of your aquarium water every 5 minutes for 30–45 minutes (or use a slow drip line). Finally, gently net the fish and introduce it into the tank. Do not dump travel bag water into your main aquarium.
