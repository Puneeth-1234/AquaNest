Care & Wellbeing

Aquarium Health Essentials

Setting up a thriving aquatic ecosystem requires attention to water chemistry and gradual acclimatization.

Water Chemistry (pH & Temp)

Maintain stable parameters rather than chasing exact figures. Most tropical fish thrive in 24°C–27°C with a neutral pH of 6.8–7.5. Always dechlorinate fresh tap water.

Drip Acclimatization

Float the insulated travel bag in your aquarium for 20 minutes to equalize temperature. Gradually add small cups of tank water over 45 minutes before gently releasing your fish.

Nutritious Feeding Regimen

Feed only what your fish can consume in 2 minutes, twice daily. Alternate between high-protein sinking pellets, freeze-dried bloodworms, and spirulina flakes.

Tank Mate Compatibility

Pair active schoolers like Tetras and Guppies with calm tank mates. Keep male Bettas solitary or in peaceful planted desktop setups with nerite snails.
